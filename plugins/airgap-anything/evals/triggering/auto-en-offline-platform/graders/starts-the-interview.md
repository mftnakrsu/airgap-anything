The agent starts a design interview instead of answering from memory. Its reply asks the user at least one question about their organization, workload or hardware (for example how many people or departments, which models, what GPUs or servers exist), or says that it will ask such questions one at a time. It does not present a finished architecture, a list of component versions, or deployment files without asking first. A reply in the same language as the request is expected.
